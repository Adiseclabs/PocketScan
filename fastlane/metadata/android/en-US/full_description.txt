PocketLab is a small contribution to the open-source community by Aditya Bhosale: free forever, no paid servers, no telemetry.

- LAN scanner: finds devices on your Wi-Fi, grabs service banners, scores risk
- New-device alerts with a foreground monitor
- BLE tracker detection (AirTag, SmartTag, Tile, Chipolo, Pebblebee) and Fox Hunt with haptics
- Offline CVE hints (heuristic; banners ignore distro backports)
- Advisory-only blocklists (StevenBlack, Phishing Army, URLhaus, OpenPhish) - nothing is blocked

No root. No VPN. No firewall. No packet capture. Everything runs on your device.
